Project: Arzi (अर्ज़ी)
Event: BharatCode Build League
Direction: Everyday assistance

What it does:
Arzi helps people in India write formal complaints and applications. You describe
your problem in Hindi, English, Hinglish or another Indian language. Arzi picks the
right document (RTI application, RTI first appeal, bank complaint, consumer notice,
or civic/utility complaint), asks for the missing details, drafts the letter in the
language you choose, checks it, and gives a step-by-step "what to do next" list.

How BharatCode is used:
- Model API: BharatCode chat completions (default model: qwen-3.8-27b)
- Call 1 (intake): classifies the problem and extracts details already given
- Call 2 (drafting): writes the formal document from verified rules and the user's facts
- Fees, deadlines, legal sections and portals come from a human-checked knowledge
  file (src/lib/documents.ts), not from the model
